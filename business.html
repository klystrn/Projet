Why assessments alone don't fix it

Take-homes broke the same week résumés did.

Anything that can be typed into a prompt can be produced by a model. So we stopped asking whether AI was used, and started asking whether the candidate can defend it.

Everyone else

The defense

You cannot walk code
you did not write.

Someone who used AI well justifies the tradeoffs and extends the work on the spot. Someone who pasted the output stalls on their own files. You'll know the difference inside five minutes.
