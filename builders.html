The defense

Using AI is fine.
Not understanding it isn't.

If you used AI well, you can justify the tradeoffs and extend the work on the spot — that's a strength, not something to hide. If you just pasted the output, you'll stall on your own files. The defense only rewards the first kind of builder.

Auto-fail

Can't explain your own core files. Contradicts your own submission.

Projet writes the rubric. The hiring manager runs the call — this is your 15 minutes to prove ownership, not just output.
