Why assessments alone don't fix it

Take-homes broke the same week resumes did.

Anything that can be typed into a prompt can be produced by a model. So we stopped asking whether AI was used.

Everyone else

Score the artifact.

A submission proves an artifact exists. It says nothing about who produced it, and scoring it harder only raises the cost of the same guess.

Projet

Make them defend it.

The submission buys 15 minutes with the hiring manager, on a rubric we supply. An AI interviewer would just be one machine questioning another.

The submission is the ticket · the defense is the decision
How it works

One backlog. Five steps. A real hire.

01

Design

Company posts a real challenge, project, or task from its own backlog.

02

Define

Rubric scoring, plus exactly what the hiring manager is looking for.

03

Score

Submitted materials are reviewed anonymously against the rubric.

04

Review

Scores, feedback, summary, CV, and portfolio reach the reviewer together.

05

Hire!

Save months of onboarding — and the cost of onboarding the wrong person.

The company's own backlog becomes the assessment. Nothing about the candidate reaches the reviewer until the work has been defended.

The defense

You cannot walk code
you did not write.

Someone who used AI well justifies the tradeoffs and extends the work on the spot. Someone who pasted the output stalls on their own files.

Auto-fail

Cannot explain their own core files. Contradicts their own submission.

Projet writes the rubric. The employer runs the call — so the judgement stays where the risk sits.
