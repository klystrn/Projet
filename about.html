About us

Hiring on proof,
not paper.

Projet is a Singapore-based proof-based hiring platform. Companies post a real problem from their own backlog, builders submit real work against it, and hiring decisions get made on what candidates actually built, not what they wrote about themselves.

The problem

Anyone can write a good resume. AI can write a great one.

A polished CV or portfolio no longer tells a hiring team much on its own. AI can produce a convincing one in minutes, and it says nothing about whether the person behind it can actually do the job. Companies end up screening on how well someone describes their work, not what they built, and builders without a name brand on their resume get filtered out before anyone looks at what they can do.

Our mission

Replace credentials with proof.

Projet gives companies a real problem from their own backlog to post as a brief, and gives builders a fair shot to show what they can do by actually doing it. Every submission is scored and ranked before a company ever sees a name, so the work speaks first, not the resume attached to it.

Our vision

Hiring on proof, as the default.

We want the strongest signal in a hiring decision to be a real thing someone built, not a polished summary of it, and not who they know. As more of what candidates submit can be AI-generated, that only gets more important, not less. Projet is building toward proof-based hiring being the normal way companies find talent, not a pilot program.
