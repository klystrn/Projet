How it works

Five steps from brief to hire.

STEP 01

Post & apply

A company publishes a real brief from its own backlog. Builders apply to take it on.

STEP 02

Async submission

Builders do the work on their own time and submit it anonymously — no name, no school, no CV attached.

STEP 03

Live 15-min defense

The part AI can't sit in for. Candidates walk through their decisions live and answer for them under questioning.

STEP 04

Rubric scoring

Scored against a fixed rubric, so every candidate is judged on the same four things. Pass mark is 3.5 out of 5.

Ownership 40% Technical depth 25% Live navigation 20% Communication 15%
STEP 05

Hire decision

The company decides with evidence in hand — work it can see, reasoning it heard defended live.
